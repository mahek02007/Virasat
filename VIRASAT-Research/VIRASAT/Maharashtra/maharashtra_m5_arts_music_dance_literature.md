# Maharashtra Cultural Heritage Knowledge Base
## Module 5: Arts, Crafts, Music, Dance, Literature & Folklore

### PART 12: Arts & Crafts

#### Warli Painting
* **Origin/Region**: Palghar district (specifically Dahanu, Talasari, Jawhar, Palghar, Mokhada, Vikramgad).
* **Community**: Warli tribe, mainly women traditionally, though men participate heavily now.
* **Materials & Techniques**: Rice paste (white) on a mud-coated background (geru). Bamboo sticks are used as brushes.
* **Motifs/Symbols**: Circles (sun/moon), triangles (mountains/trees), squares (chauk - sacred enclosure), Tarpa dance, hunting scenes.
* **Significance**: Originally ritualistic (weddings, harvests).
* **Modern Evolution**: Commercialized on paper/canvas. Jivya Soma Mashe popularized it globally.
* **Recognition**: GI Tagged. [HIGH CONFIDENCE] [Source: Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Govt of India]

#### Paithani Weaving
* **Origin/Region**: Paithan and Yeola.
* **Technique**: Kadiyal technique (interlocking tapestry).
* **Motifs**: Asawali (vine/flower), Bangadi-mor (peacock in bangle), Tota-maina (parrot-mynah), Kamal (lotus).
* **Significance**: Essential bridal trousseau. 'Maha-Vastra' of Maharashtra.
* **Recognition**: GI Tagged. [HIGH CONFIDENCE] [Source: Handlooms Board, India]

#### Kolhapuri Chappals
* **Region**: Kolhapur district.
* **Technique**: Hand-crafted, vegetable-dyed leather craft. Stitched with leather cords, no nails used.
* **Recognition**: GI Tagged. [Source: Patent Design and Trademarks Office, India]

#### Sawantwadi Crafts
* **Region**: Sindhudurg district.
* **Crafts**: Wooden toys, Ganjifa cards (circular playing cards), lacquerware (chess sets, fruits).
* **Current Status**: Struggling but supported by royal family descendants and MTDC.

#### Pinguli Chitrakatha
* **Region**: Pinguli village, Sindhudurg.
* **Community**: Thakar community.
* **Technique**: Broad leaves/paper paintings depicting Ramayana/Mahabharata, accompanied by oral storytelling (leather puppetry/string puppetry).
* **Status**: Endangered. [SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION]

### PART 13: Music

#### Hindustani Classical
* **Gharanas**: Maharashtra has been a stronghold for Kirana Gharana (Bhimsen Joshi) and Jaipur-Atrauli Gharana (Kishori Amonkar). Kumar Gandharva also had strong ties.
* **Natya Sangeet**: Unique to Marathi theatre, classical and semi-classical music integrated into plays (e.g., Bal Gandharva).

#### Folk & Devotional Traditions
* **Abhang & Bhajan**: Devotional poetry set to music, popularised by Varkari sect. Singers like Bhimsen Joshi rendered them classically.
* **Powada**: Warrior ballads praising bravery (e.g., Agnidas's powada on Afzal Khan's death).
* **Lavani Music**: Characterized by the 14-beat Dhadya taal, energetic, Dholki-driven.
* **Instruments**: Dholki, Sambal, Halgi, Tasha, Tutari, Ektara, Taal.

### PART 14: Dance & Performance

#### Lavani & Tamasha
* **Lavani**: Two main types - Nirguni (philosophical) and Shringari (erotic). Formats include Dholki Farad (fast-paced public) and Baithakichi (seated, intimate, expression-focused).
* **Performers**: Mangala Bansode, Yamunabai Waikar.
* **Tamasha**: Travelling folk theatre. Components: Gan (invocation), Gondhal, Batavya (comedy), Vag (main play/script).
* **Community**: Traditionally Mahar, Mang, Kolhati communities.

#### Other Folk Dances
* **Koli Dance**: Fisherfolk dance from coastal Konkan. Movements mimic rowing and fishing.
* **Dhangari Gaja**: Shepherd dance, honors Biruba, uses heavy drumming.
* **Lezim**: Group exercise/dance with a jingling wooden instrument (lezim). Core part of Ganesh festival processions.
* **Gondhal**: Ritual performance dedicated to Goddesses (Tulja Bhavani, Renuka).

### PART 15: Literature

#### Classical & Bhakti Literature
* **Inscriptions**: Shravanabelagola inscription (oldest Marathi epigraphy).
* **Bhakti Era**: 
  - Dnyaneshwar (Dnyaneshwari - commentary on Gita).
  - Tukaram (Abhangas).
  - Namdev, Eknath (Bharuds).
  - Women saints: Muktabai, Janabai.

#### Modern & Dalit Literature
* **19th Century Reform**: Mahatma Jyotirao Phule (Gulamgiri).
* **Modern Era**: V.S. Khandekar (Jnanpith winner), P.L. Deshpande ('Pula' - humor/satire), Shanta Shelke.
* **Dalit Literature**: Daya Pawar (Baluta - first Dalit autobiography), Namdeo Dhasal (Golpitha - poetry), Baburao Bagul. Themes of caste oppression, assertion, and humanity.

### PART 16: Folklore, Stories & Legends

#### Fort Legends [FOLK TRADITION] [HISTORICAL MYTH]
* **Sinhagad (Tanaji Malusare)**: Legend says Tanaji used a monitor lizard (ghorpad) named Yashwanti to scale the fort. [SCHOLARLY INTERPRETATION: Often debated if the lizard was real or a metaphor for climbing ropes/hooks].
* **Pratapgad**: Stories of Shivaji Maharaj's encounter with Afzal Khan are embedded in folklore via Powadas, blending history with heroic myth.

#### Temple Legends [RELIGIOUS BELIEF]
* **Ashtavinayak**: Origin stories of the eight self-manifested (Swayambhu) Ganesha temples. E.g., Mayureshwar at Morgaon (Ganesha riding a peacock to defeat Sindhu).

#### Tribal Stories
* **Warli Cosmology**: Creation myths involving Kansari (goddess of grain) and Palghat (marriage goddess).
